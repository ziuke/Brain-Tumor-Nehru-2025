About Brain Tumor Detection

Upload your brain MRI scan image here for automated tumor detection and classification. The system uses advanced deep learning models to analyze your scan and provide predictions.

Accepted Formats
  • Image formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB
  • Make sure the image is a clear brain MRI scan for best results
What We Detect
  • Glioma: A type of tumor that occurs in the brain
  • Meningioma: Tumors that arise from the meninges
  • Pituitary: Tumors in the pituitary gland
  • No Tumor: Normal brain scan with no tumor detected
