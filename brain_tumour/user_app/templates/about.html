About Us

AI-Powered Brain Tumour Detection

At Brain Tumour AI, our mission is to harness artificial intelligence and medical imaging to aid in early tumour diagnosis. Our algorithms are trained on thousands of MRI images, capable of classifying tumour types and predicting malignancy with remarkable precision.

We collaborate with healthcare experts and institutions to deliver a system that is both clinically reliable and user-friendly. Whether you’re a medical professional or a researcher, Brain Tumour AI empowers you to explore, analyze, and interpret MRI scans more effectively.
